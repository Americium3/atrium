  beside this file, as that licence requires.
  https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwHeartSerif

  Two conditions worth stating plainly, because they bind what this repo may
  DO with the file rather than only what it must ship:

  * The file is redistributed byte-for-byte. Subsetting it — the obvious
    optimization for an 11 MB face — produces a "Derived Program" under this
    licence, which must then be renamed and shipped with its source. So the
    hall carries the whole font or it carries none of it.
  * The font keeps its name, and the licence travels with the file.

  It replaced Noto Serif SC (OFL, 25 MB): a modern Songti in a hall that is
  engraved Art-Deco and wanted a warmer Mincho next to EB Garamond.
